About us

The Beginning

Why we keep farms the way libraries keep first editions — slowly, honestly and for good.

Years before this platform had a name, someone planted mango trees on a gentle slope in the Nilgiris foothills. The trees grew. The stream kept its course. And when we found the orchard, we made a decision that became our founding rule: we would never sell a farm whose story we couldn't tell in full.

The managed farmland category is full of promises — renders of orchards that don't exist yet, communities that never gather, reports that stop arriving after registration. We decided to be the opposite of that. This is not a promise. These mangoes exist. The trees are decades old and already in harvest. The families are real. The documents are open.

Founded in Coimbatore, The Farm Stories finds the most soulful land in South India and keeps it like a book — verified before it is listed, documented every month after it is sold, and shared by a small circle of families who know each other's names. Mango Meadows in Agali is our first promise made real. More are being read.

We would rather lose a sale than lose your trust. That single sentence is our entire business plan.

01

Curated land

Every project starts with the land itself. We look for natural character — mature trees, clean air, elevation, water that holds through the seasons. The land must already have a soul before we put our name near it.

02

Boutique scale

We cap every community at a small circle of families. Small enough to know your neighbours; large enough to share a pavilion, a trail, and a harvest evening together. Genuine community is impossible at scale.

03

Documentation trust

Clear title, clean patta, proper EC, registered layout — shown before you ask, not after. Legal clarity is our brand promise, and the monthly record keeps that promise long after registration day.
